# cr-character-design-refs

Goal (an earlier round): look at the official card art for all 25 deck
cards to guide our own original low-poly 3D character models.

## What happened
- That round committed a folder of official card images plus a short
  per-character design write-up, as visual reference only. The game never
  used them: it draws all of its own characters, and no build, test or tool
  read that folder.

## Removed (Stage 0, 2026-11-16)
- The reference images and their write-ups were removed from the project.
  Nothing in the game, its tests or its tools changed.
- Do not add official game art to the repository again. Design notes for
  our own characters live in the art notes in this folder.
