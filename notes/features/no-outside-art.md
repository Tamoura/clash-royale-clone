# No art from other games

- The repository holds only the game's own art: procedural drawings and
  models, and the CC0 packs listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- Do not add another game's art, screenshots or card pictures, not even as
  reference. Stage 0 (2026-11-16) removed a folder of reference card
  pictures an early round had committed; nothing in the game, its build or
  its tests used it.
- Design notes for the game's own characters live in the art notes in this
  folder (`art-direction-2026-11.md`, `art-cast-kit.md`).
